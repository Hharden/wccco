## 1. How to Choose PNW Rainwear: Waterproofing & Layering

Before selecting gear, understand the three layers necessary for West Coast weather:

1. **Base Layer:** Wicks moisture away from your skin to prevent damp chills (e.g., Merino wool or quick-dry synthetics).
2. **Mid-Layer:** Traps body heat without adding excess bulk (e.g., heavy fleece or lightweight insulated sweaters).
3. **Outer Shell:** Shields against wind and torrential rain using waterproof membranes and fully sealed seams.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What hydrostatic head rating do I need for PNW rain?
For light drizzle, 5,000mm to 10,000mm is sufficient. For continuous downpours or heavy hiking in wet brush, look for ratings of 15,000mm to 20,000mm+.

### Is water-resistant the same as waterproof?
No. Water-resistant fabrics repel light moisture temporarily, while waterproof fabrics utilize sealed seams and hydrophobic membranes to block heavy rain entirely.

### How do I maintain the waterproof DWR coating on my rain shell?
Wash your jacket with a specialized technical cleaner (like Nikwax Tech Wash) and tumble dry on low heat to reactivate the Durable Water Repellent (DWR) finish.
